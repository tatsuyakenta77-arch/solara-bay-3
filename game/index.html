An open-world crime story in Port Meridian

Jace Marlowe came back to Port Meridian to drive one last job for fixer Mara Quinn. Steal cars, outrun the PD, settle scores at the docks — and don't get busted.

On foot: WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, E enter a car, F punch, mouse click shoot, drag mouse to look.

Driving: W accelerate, S brake / reverse, A/D steer, Space handbrake, E get out.

Rules: stealing cars, hitting people and shooting raises your wanted level. Cops chase you — stay out of sight and the heat cools off. Get busted and you pay a fine. Finish all 6 missions to own the city. Progress saves automatically.

Esc pauses · M mutes the sound.

An original game. No real city, person or brand is depicted. Best in desktop Chrome.
